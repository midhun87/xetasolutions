Our Story

Built on a Passion for Plant Science

Geeky Researcher began with the shared vision of two students, Nirmal Raj and N.H. Shankar Reddy, who wanted to make quality knowledge more accessible.

What started as a learning initiative in Plant Science and Plant Pathology grew into a focused educational platform offering concept-driven lectures, examination preparation, revision, and research-oriented learning.

Mission: To create a focused, reliable, and academically strong learning ecosystem exclusively for Plant Pathology.
