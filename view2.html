Our Story

A Vision Rooted in Plant Science

Geeky Researcher began with the shared vision of Nirmal Raj and N.H. Shankar Reddy to make quality knowledge more accessible.

What started as an educational initiative in Plant Science and Plant Pathology developed into a focused learning platform built around conceptual understanding, competitive examination preparation, and research-oriented learning.
